Group order rules

Please read before joining a GO.

Claiming items
  • Claim items directly on this website — open Group orders, pick your item(s) and member/version, and submit with your Instagram handle.
  • A set is secured once 2/3 of its spots are claimed. You can check whether your claim is secured anytime under My orders.
Securing your claim
  • After the preorder closes, I'll create a group chat to collect payment and announce the payment deadline.
  • Only secured claims are ordered. If a claim isn't secured, it won't be charged.
Payment
  • You only pay for your secured items — unsecured spots aren't charged.
  • Send payment using one of the four methods below, then paste your transaction ID on the website (My orders → Pay) so I can confirm it.
  • Paying for more than one GO at once? Send a single transfer and paste the same transaction ID on each GO's payment.
  • If you pay more than your secured total, the extra becomes credit toward your other or future secured claims in this GO.
  • If a claim isn't secured by the time the GO closes, you'll be refunded for it.
  • Payment is due by the deadline announced in the group chat. If you need an extension, message me in advance — otherwise your spot may be released.
  • No backing out without a replacement.
Payment details
  • Initial payment — covers the cost of the item. This is all that's due for now.
  • Shipping payment — covers EMS (international) and domestic shipping. Once your items arrive to me and you request a shipment (My orders → Request shipping), I'll collect the shipping fee.
Payment methods
PayPal F&F (no note): @jinghanc Venmo: @Jinghan-Cui Zelle: jhcui1818@gmail.com Wise: @jinghanc3
Shipping fees
  • EMS (international, to me) — $0.50 per card; other items depend on size and weight.
  • Domestic (within U.S.) — stamped from $1.70, tracked from $6.
  • International joiners — stamped from $2.70; tracked depends on the country.
  • Fees are calculated based on the item's weight and dimensions.
Shipping timeline
  • International shipping takes approximately 3 weeks to arrive to me.
  • Once I receive your shipping request, I'll send you a picture of your items, then ship them within 1 week (after your shipping payment).
Communication policy
  • Please follow me after claiming so I can add you to the group chat.
  • I'll keep your order's status updated here under My orders and in the group chat.
  • If I am unable to contact you for more than two weeks, your claim will be removed.
