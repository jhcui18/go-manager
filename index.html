Group order rules

Please read before joining a GO.

Claiming items
  • Claim items directly on this website — open Group orders, pick your item(s) and member/version, and submit with your Instagram handle.
  • Set-based items — cards come in complete member sets, and you claim the exact members you want. A set is secured once 2/3 of its spots are claimed, and the whole set is ordered together. Empty spots in an already-secured set stay claimable even after the item closes.
  • Batch-based items — no full set needed: claim any members you like, repeats welcome (where noted, your member pick only sets sorting priority). Claims are pooled first come, first served into fixed-size batches — the batch size is shown on the item — and each batch is ordered once it fills.
  • You can check whether your claim is secured anytime under My orders.
Securing your claim
  • Each GO's close time and payment due date are posted here on the website — and some items have their own close/due dates, shown on the item itself.
  • After the preorder closes, I'll create a group chat to collect payment.
  • Only secured claims are ordered. If a claim isn't secured, it won't be charged.
Payment
  • You only pay for your secured items — unsecured spots aren't charged.
  • Send payment using one of the four methods below, then paste your transaction ID on the website (My orders → Pay) so I can confirm it. Zelle doesn't show a transaction ID — leave it blank or use your name.
  • Paying for more than one GO at once? Send a single transfer and paste the same transaction ID on each GO's payment.
  • If you pay more than your secured total, the extra becomes credit toward your other or future secured claims in this GO.
  • If a claim isn't secured by the time the GO closes, you'll be refunded for it.
  • Payment is due by the date posted on the website — check your items under My orders, and note that some items have their own due dates. If you need an extension, message me in advance — otherwise your spot may be released.
  • If your claim is secured after the payment due date has passed (items can close before payment is due, and spots can secure late), payment is due as soon as possible.
  • No backing out without a replacement.
Payment details
  • Initial payment — covers the cost of the item. This is all that's due for now.
  • Shipping payment — covers EMS (international) and domestic shipping. Once your items arrive to me and you request a shipment (My orders → Request shipping), I'll collect the shipping fee.

Shipping fees
  • EMS (international, to me) — $0.65 per card; other items depend on size and weight.
  • Domestic (within U.S.) — stamped from $1.80 (up to 4 photocards, no tracking) or tracked from $6. You pick the method on the shipping request form.
  • International joiners — stamped from $2.80 (no card limit) or tracked; tracked cost depends on your country.
  • Bulky items (albums, merch) always ship tracked, whichever option you pick.
  • Fees are calculated based on the item's weight and dimensions.
Requesting your shipment
  • 🐱 I have a cat — items are stored and packed in my home. If you have a severe cat allergy, please keep this in mind before joining.
  • International shipping takes approximately 3 weeks to arrive to me.
  • When your items are paid and marked Ready, a Request shipping form appears under My orders. Pick stamped or tracked, and feel free to combine GO claims and Shop orders in one package.
  • Once I receive your shipping request, I'll send you a picture of your items and let you know the shipping fee; your package goes out after the shipping payment.
  • Follow your package under My orders → Your shipments — for tracked shipments, your tracking number appears there and clicking it opens live tracking.
Shop
  • The Shop tab sells leftover photocards, merch & albums — first come, first served, no securing needed.
  • After placing an order, pay under My orders with the same payment methods as GOs.
  • Shop orders ship like everything else — on their own, or bundled with your GO claims in one shipment to save on fees.
Communication policy
  • Please follow me after claiming so I can add you to the group chat.
  • I'll keep your order's status updated here under My orders and in the group chat.
  • If I am unable to contact you for more than two weeks, your claim will be removed.
  • Blocking policy — you will be blocked from future GOs if you back out without a replacement, are unresponsive, or don't send payment after I reach out multiple times.
