Group order rules

Please read before joining a GO.

Claiming items
  • To claim an item, comment on the post. I will reply to confirm your claim.
  • A set will be secured once 2/3 of the spots are claimed. I'll regularly update the post to show how many sets are secured.
Securing your claim
  • I will create a group chat at the end of the preorder period.
  • If your claim is secured, you will be added to the group chat. If not, it means your claim was not secured.
Group chat & payment
  • The group chat is used to collect payment and provide updates on shipping timelines and item status.
  • Payment is due within one week after the group chat is created. If you need an extension, message me in advance — otherwise your spot will be released.
  • No backing out without replacement.
Payment details
  • Initial payment — covers the cost of the item.
  • Shipping payment — covers international and domestic shipping, collected after I receive the items.
Payment methods
PayPal F&F (no note): @jinghanc Venmo: @Jinghan-Cui Zelle: jhcui1818@gmail.com Wise: @jinghanc3
Shipping fees (domestic, within U.S.)
  • Tracked shipping — starts at $4.60
  • Stamped shipping — starts at $1.50 (limit 2 cards or fewer)
  • Fees are calculated based on the item's weight and dimensions.
Shipping timeline
  • International shipping takes approximately 3 weeks to arrive to me.
  • Once I have the items in hand, I will ship them within 3 days after receiving your shipping payment.
Communication policy
  • Please follow me after joining the GO so I can reach you.
  • If I am unable to contact you for more than two weeks, your claim will be removed.
  • I will send you a picture of your claimed items for confirmation before I ship them.
